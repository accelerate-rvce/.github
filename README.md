## About Us

**Accelerate** is the technical innovation club of the **Department of Computer Science and Engineering at RV College of Engineering, Bengaluru**.

We exist to build a culture of creativity, collaboration, and hands-on problem-solving among students — turning classroom concepts into real, shippable work.

Since our inception, we've organized and participated in **hackathons, bootcamps, workshops, and student-led innovation initiatives**, giving students at every level a platform to explore technology and build practical skills beyond the syllabus.

> Everything we build comes back to our name — **helping people and ideas move faster.**

## 🌐 Our Technical Ecosystem

Accelerate is more than events. We are building an ecosystem where students can **learn, build, collaborate, and contribute**.

Our GitHub organization brings together:

- 🔧 **Open-source projects**
- 🎨 **Reusable UI components & design systems**
- 🚀 **Hackathon and event infrastructure**
- 📚 **Learning resources & technical documentation**
- 🧪 **Experimental and student-led projects**
- 🤝 **Collaborative development initiatives**

Whether you're contributing your first pull request or building your next ambitious project, there's a place to build with us.

<br/>

## 🙌 Join Accelerate

Accelerate welcomes students from the **CSE community at RVCE**, regardless of prior experience.

Whether you're interested in **development, AI/ML, cybersecurity, competitive programming, design, or emerging technologies**, you can learn, collaborate, and build alongside other students.

We conduct inductions at the beginning of each academic year and welcome contributors to our open-source projects throughout the year.
